## What the game stores on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, coins, boosters, hearts, settings) and an
anonymous, randomly generated **Support ID** are saved in your browser's or
app's local storage on your device. Nothing here is sent to us. You can erase
your progress at any time in Settings → Reset progress; your Support ID and
settings are kept so support codes keep working.

## What leaves your device

**Anonymous usage analytics.** To find levels that are too hard, features
nobody uses and bugs that crash the game, ChromaFlask sends small event
records to PostHog, Inc. (servers in the United States). Each record contains
the event name (for example "level_complete"), game details such as the level
number, move count and stars, and your Support ID so events from the same
install can be counted together. We do **not** collect your name, email
address, contacts, location, advertising ID or any device identifier, and we
do not use this data for advertising. PostHog processes it on our behalf under
its data processing agreement.

You choose this when you first set up your profile, and you can change it at
any time in **Settings → Share anonymous usage data**. While it is off, no
event leaves your device.

**Error reports.** If the game crashes, a short description of the error and
the same game details above are sent through the same channel, subject to the
same setting.

**Purchases.** In the Google Play and App Store versions, purchases are
handled entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. We
receive a purchase token to deliver what you bought; we never see your payment
details.

**Support email.** If you email us, we receive whatever you send, including
your Support ID. We use it only to help you and keep it only as long as needed.

## Children

ChromaFlask is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct or
delete data about you. Because analytics are keyed only by your Support ID,
include it (Settings → Help & support) when you write to [SUPPORT_EMAIL] and
we will delete the events recorded under it.

We will update this page when the game's data practices change and update the
